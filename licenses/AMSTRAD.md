# Amstrad hardware identification

Updated 2026-09-09. ZEsarPCW uses a project-authored GPLv3
[native C bootstrap](../docs/BOOTSTRAP.md). Amstrad firmware is not included,
and the archived Amstrad ROM redistribution statement is no longer a licence
dependency of this distribution.

Amstrad and PCW identify the emulated hardware. The Amstrad logo remains on the
on-screen keyboard as a depiction of that hardware. ZEsarPCW is independent
and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the hardware
manufacturer or trademark holders. The project does not claim that the marks
have expired or assign their present ownership to an unverified company.
Its software licences do not grant rights to third-party trademarks.
